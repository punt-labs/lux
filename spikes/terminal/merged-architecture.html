Lux + biff + vox as one system: layers and domains

Three layers (peer fabric, per-host server, human surface) and seven domains. A domain keeps its own model, protocol and owner even where several share a process. Process boundaries are dashed; they are the part most likely to change and the least important.

What this settles for the terminal, and what it does not

Settled: process hosting is a Layer 2 domain, beside content and communication, not a client of them and not a separate tier. Its north interface is the stream channel to the surface; its east interface is the content domain (a session is shown by a scene element, read by inspect_scene); its policy comes from identity & trust. It has no south interface: terminal bytes never reach the fabric.

Not settled, and decided by you, not by this diagram: (1) whether Layer 2 becomes one process or stays two with a shared protocol; (2) which transport Layer 3↔2 uses, since vox and lux chose differently; (3) whether "start a shell" is a row in biffd's M3 trust table or a separate consent regime; (4) the order of work: biffd M1 first, or the process-hosting domain first against today's luxd.
