\section{Introduction}
% ============================================================

This is the fidelity control for H2 in
\texttt{docs/connection\_lease\_reaping.tex}, not the design. It removes
only the release half of \texttt{Depart}: a departing connection --
graceful disconnect or SDK idle-reap, no lease condition required -- is
still correctly dropped from \texttt{registered}, but every scene it
owned is left exactly as it was. This models
\texttt{HubDisplay.drop\_connection}'s own docstring verbatim: ``a
session's UI survives the session: the connection's roots stay installed
and stay owned by its id.'' Read the main specification first; this
document states only what differs, in Section~\ref{sec:depart}.

  identity : CONN \pfun IDENT \\
  transport : CONN \pfun TSTATE \\
  lease : CONN \pfun LSTATE \\
  owner : SCENE \fun OWNER
\where

  \dom identity = \dom transport \\
  \dom transport = \dom lease

\section{Operations}
% ============================================================

\subsection{Depart --- BUGGY: no ownership release}
\label{sec:depart}

\textbf{This is the one operation that differs from the design.} The
departing connection \texttt{c?} still correctly leaves
\texttt{registered} -- no lease condition guards it, matching the real
\texttt{drop\_connection}, which fires on an ordinary clean disconnect
regardless of lease state -- but \texttt{owner} is left completely
unchanged: no scene \texttt{c?} owned is released. Compare against the
correct \texttt{Depart} in \texttt{connection\_lease\_reaping.tex}: the
correct version clears every scene owned by \texttt{c?} to
\texttt{unowned} in the same step this one only drops \texttt{c?} from
\texttt{registered}.

\begin{schema}{Depart}
  \Delta ConnReg \\
  c? : CONN
\where
  c? \in registered \\
  registered' = registered \setminus \{ c? \} \\
  owner' = owner \\
  identity' = identity \\
  transport' = transport \\
  lease' = lease
\end{schema}

\subsection{Connect, Renew, TransportDies, LeaseLapse, Read, TimedReap, Claim --- unchanged}

Identical to the main specification: only \texttt{Depart}'s
ownership-release step is removed. \texttt{TimedReap} in particular is
the \emph{correct}, releasing coordinator -- this control isolates the
H2 finding to \texttt{Depart}'s own graceful-disconnect path, so the
background backstop must stay intact here exactly as it is in the fixed
spec.

\begin{schema}{Connect}
  \Delta ConnReg \\
  c? : CONN \\
  i? : IDENT
\where
  registered' = registered \cup \{ c? \} \\
  identity' = identity \oplus \{ c? \mapsto i? \} \\
  transport' = transport \oplus \{ c? \mapsto tup \} \\
  lease' = lease \oplus \{ c? \mapsto llive \} \\
  owner' = owner
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{Renew}
  \Delta ConnReg \\
  c? : CONN
\where
  c? \in registered \\
  transport~c? = tup \\
  lease' = lease \oplus \{ c? \mapsto llive \} \\
  registered' = registered \\
  identity' = identity \\
  transport' = transport \\
  owner' = owner
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{TransportDies}
  \Delta ConnReg \\
  c? : CONN
\where
  c? \in registered \\
  transport~c? = tup \\
  transport' = transport \oplus \{ c? \mapsto tdown \} \\
  registered' = registered \\
  identity' = identity \\
  lease' = lease \\
  owner' = owner
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{LeaseLapse}
  \Delta ConnReg \\
  c? : CONN
\where
  c? \in registered \\
  lease~c? = llive \\
  lease' = lease \oplus \{ c? \mapsto llapsed \} \\
  registered' = registered \\
  identity' = identity \\
  transport' = transport \\
  owner' = owner
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{Read}
  \Xi ConnReg
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{TimedReap}
  \Delta ConnReg \\
  c? : CONN
\where
  c? \in registered \\
  lease~c? = llapsed \\
  registered' = registered \setminus \{ c? \} \\
  owner' = owner \oplus \\
  \quad~ (\{ s : SCENE | owner~s = conn(c?) \} \cross \{ unowned \}) \\
  identity' = identity \\
  transport' = transport \\
  lease' = lease
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{Claim}
  \Delta ConnReg \\
  c? : CONN \\
  s? : SCENE
\where
  c? \in registered \\
  transport~c? = tup \\
  lease' = lease \oplus \{ c? \mapsto llive \} \\
  registered' = registered \setminus \\
  \quad~ \{ c2 : CONN | c2 \in registered \land c2 \neq c? \land
    lease~c2 = llapsed \} \\
  (owner \oplus (\{ s : SCENE | \exists c2 : CONN @ \\
    \qquad c2 \in registered \land c2 \neq c? \land lease~c2 = llapsed
    \land owner~s = conn(c2) \} \\
    \qquad \cross \{ unowned \}))~s? = unowned \\
  \lor \\
  (owner \oplus (\{ s : SCENE | \exists c2 : CONN @ \\
    \qquad c2 \in registered \land c2 \neq c? \land lease~c2 = llapsed
    \land owner~s = conn(c2) \} \\
    \qquad \cross \{ unowned \}))~s? = conn(c?) \\
  owner' = (owner \oplus (\{ s : SCENE | \exists c2 : CONN @ \\
    \qquad c2 \in registered \land c2 \neq c? \land lease~c2 = llapsed
    \land owner~s = conn(c2) \} \\
    \qquad \cross \{ unowned \})) \oplus \{ s? \mapsto conn(c?) \} \\
  identity' = identity \\
  transport' = transport
\end{schema}

\section{Verification: the goal that must flip}
% ============================================================

Against this variant, I2/I5's negation must be \emph{found} --- the trace
in \texttt{connection\_lease\_reaping.tex}, Section H2, reaches it
directly, and never lapses a lease at all:
\texttt{Connect}(c1, i1); \texttt{Claim}(c1, s1); \texttt{Depart}(c1).

\begin{verbatim}
  # must be FOUND against this variant (NOT found against the fixed spec)
  probcli docs/connection_lease_reaping_no_release_buggy.tex \
    -model_check -nodead \
    -goal "#s.(#c.(s:SCENE & c:CONN & owner(s) = conn(c) &
                   not(c : registered)))" \
    -p DEFAULT_SETSIZE 3
\end{verbatim}

I3's negation must also be \emph{found}, continuing the same trace one
step further with a live reconnect under the same identity --
\texttt{Connect}(c2, i1):

\begin{verbatim}
  # must be FOUND against this variant (NOT found against the fixed spec)
  probcli docs/connection_lease_reaping_no_release_buggy.tex \
    -model_check -nodead \
    -goal "#s.(#c.(#c2.(s:SCENE & c:CONN & c2:CONN &
                        c : registered & not(c2 : registered) &
                        c2 : dom(identity) & identity(c) = identity(c2) &
                        not(c = c2) & owner(s) = conn(c2))))" \
    -p DEFAULT_SETSIZE 3
\end{verbatim}

I1 must stay unaffected --- this variant does not touch
\texttt{TransportDies} or \texttt{LeaseLapse}, so the chain
\texttt{TransportDies};\texttt{Depart} still completes exactly as in the
fixed spec (this variant does not remove \texttt{Depart}'s own guard, only
what happens to \texttt{owner} afterward):

\begin{verbatim}

  probcli docs/connection_lease_reaping_no_release_buggy.tex \
    -model_check -nodead \

    -p DEFAULT_SETSIZE 3
