\title{PTY child ownership: reap before forgetting}
\maketitle

This models one child and its sole parent waiter. A zombie retains its PID;
reaping releases it, so a later signal could target an unrelated reused PID.
The Python owner must never signal after reaping, and the session core must
retain ownership if termination fails. External waiters are excluded: the
daemon is single-threaded and exclusively owns its children.

\begin{zed}
LIFE ::= active | zombie | gone
\end{zed}
\begin{zed}
FLAG ::= yes | no
\end{zed}
\begin{schema}{Child}
life : LIFE \\
reaped : FLAG \\
masterOpen : FLAG \\
tracked : FLAG \\
signals : \power LIFE
\where
reaped = yes \implies life = gone
\end{schema}
\begin{schema}{Init}
Child'
\where
life' = active \land reaped' = no \\
masterOpen' = yes \land tracked' = yes \land signals' = \emptyset
\end{schema}
\begin{schema}{KernelExit}
\Delta Child
\where
life = active \\
life' = zombie \land reaped' = reaped \\
masterOpen' = masterOpen \land tracked' = tracked \land signals' = signals
\end{schema}
\begin{schema}{CloseMaster}
\Delta Child
\where
masterOpen = yes \\
masterOpen' = no \land life' = life \land reaped' = reaped \\
tracked' = tracked \land signals' = signals
\end{schema}
\begin{schema}{PollReap}
\Delta Child
\where
life = zombie \land reaped = no \\
life' = gone \land reaped' = yes \\
masterOpen' = masterOpen \land tracked' = tracked \land signals' = signals
\end{schema}
\begin{schema}{SignalChild}
\Delta Child
\where
tracked = yes \land reaped = no \\
signals' = signals \cup \{life\} \\
life' = life \land reaped' = reaped \\
masterOpen' = masterOpen \land tracked' = tracked
\end{schema}
\begin{schema}{ForgetChild}
\Delta Child
\where
tracked = yes \land reaped = yes \\
tracked' = no \land life' = life \land reaped' = reaped \\
masterOpen' = masterOpen \land signals' = signals
\end{schema}
\begin{schema}{Idle}
\Xi Child
\end{schema}

Search for the safety violations, which are not state predicates:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{gone:signals}: a signal targets a released PID.
\item \texttt{tracked=no \& life/=gone}: a live child is forgotten.
\end{itemize}

Fidelity checks remove the reap guard from SignalChild and ForgetChild
respectively. The first must find a signal after PollReap; the second must
find ownership discarded before KernelExit. This proves safety under the
sole-waiter assumption, not eventual scheduling, bounded kernel response,
or cleanup of deliberately detached grandchildren.
